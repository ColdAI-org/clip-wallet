# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is ours; the linked text is the full code and applies in full.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Clip Wallet to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers; a wallet has a lot of moving parts.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, unwelcome advances, trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information (including wallet addresses tied to a
  real identity) without their permission.
- Scams, phishing, "support" impersonation, or asking anyone for a recovery phrase or private key, ever.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when someone represents the
project in public. Report incidents to the maintainers at **[shayan@coldai.org](mailto:shayan@coldai.org)** (subject "Code of
Conduct") or privately through a maintainer. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.
Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent
ban, according to impact.
